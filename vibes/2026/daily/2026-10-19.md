## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The cost layer is tightening fast — DeepSeek's sub-dollar search pricing and Kimi K3 vLLM optimization both point to a race to make inference so cheap it becomes a rounding error, which reshapes every business model built on API margins. The interesting question is whether cheap hosted inference kills the on-device case or just raises the bar for what "worth running locally" means.
- [Hyper Optimizing Kimi K3 in vLLM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50140784) — 20pts — low score but technically dense; the specific vLLM tuning decisions here are worth reading if you're running inference at any scale

**agent-systems**: Latent-space reasoning is this week's architectural conversation — the "Hybrid Reasoning Agents" paper at the top of the front page argues for agents that reason in embedding space and only surface to text when acting, which is a meaningful departure from chain-of-thought orthodoxy. Meanwhile builders are shipping lightweight orchestration (Pi-cowboy) suggesting the framework layer is still unsettled.
- [Hybrid Reasoning Agents: LLM agents that think in latent space, act through text](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50145337) — 155pts — the core claim (reasoning doesn't need to be legible to be effective) will either look prescient or like a dead end in 18 months; worth reading now to form a view

**Deep dives**:
- [You could have invented transformers from memory](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50122878) — 621pts — the rare piece that makes a complex architecture feel *inevitable* rather than discovered; essential reading if you teach or write about ML
- [RepRapMicron](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141076) — 321pts — sub-micrometer open-source 3D motion platform is the kind of grassroots hardware project that eventually unlocks entirely new categories of maker capability; worth watching the repo
- [macOS 15.8.2 Breaks OpenGL](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141909) — 232pts 241c — not just a bug report; the comment thread is a map of everything that depends on OpenGL that Apple apparently didn't test, useful for assessing your own exposure
