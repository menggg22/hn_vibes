## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The Python JIT removal story is a genuine infrastructure regression that matters to anyone running inference or numeric workloads on CPython, and it's colliding on the same day as Nvidia's open multilingual ASR drop — the story of the day is what the runtime layer underneath ML actually looks like in 2026. On-device is getting more serious: Survival LLM (516pts) is explicitly framed as an offline emergency library, signaling that "runs without internet" is becoming a real product category.
- [Python's experimental JIT to be removed](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50257419) — 562pts — worth reading to understand whether CPython's performance trajectory can support the inference workloads being pushed onto it
- [Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe: open ASR for 40 languages](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50275128) — 106pts — open, multilingual, from Nvidia; the ASR commoditization curve is steeper than most people tracking LLMs realize
- [Survival LLM: AI assistant and emergency library that runs on your own computer](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50264853) — 516pts — on-device positioning as resilience infrastructure, not just privacy; a new and expanding frame

**agent-systems**: Two very different agent stories are ranking simultaneously — Claude being used for drug development (321pts, 331c) and a generic Agent SDK (89pts, 107c) — suggesting the field is splitting between high-stakes vertical applications and horizontal tooling. The drug development thread has comments-to-score parity, meaning it's genuinely contested, not just upvoted.
- [Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50265609) — 321pts — the debate about whether this is real science or impressive autocomplete is exactly the right argument to be having right now
- [The Agent SDK](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50273731) — 89pts — high comment ratio for its score suggests practitioners have strong opinions about the abstraction choices
- [Dayjob: Tax – evaluating AI agents on 50 professional tax assignments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50275626) — 189pts — rigorous benchmark-style evaluation of agents on a domain with ground truth; rarer and more useful than most agent posts

**Deep dives**:
- [Why is this fingerprinting script creating bioweapons?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50276998) — the title alone demands reading; if accurate it's the most alarming AI misuse story of the month
- [About the compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50271685) — the technical companion to the Apple git story; read both together to understand the actual attack surface
