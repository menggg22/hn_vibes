## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The Kimi K3.1 Pro drop is the dominant signal — a high-speed inference variant alongside a standard model suggests the infra layer is now competing on latency tiers, not just benchmark scores. GPU unlocking on consumer Nvidia cards is quietly enabling a shadow fleet of fine-tuning capacity outside the cloud.
- [GPU-unlock: FP/BF16 instruction issue rate on Nvidia 3000's cards](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50219700) — 169pts — worth reading for the microarchitecture detail on how consumer GPUs are being coaxed into training-class throughput; has implications for anyone building on-prem ML infra cheaply

**agent-systems**: The agent layer is fragmenting fast — Corgi (terminal multiplexer), Omnara (Claude Managed Agents alternative), and Argus (agentic security ops) all landed within days of each other, each picking a different host environment. The question of where agents live — terminal, browser, security console — is very much open.
- [Show HN: Omnara – Open-source alternative to Claude Managed Agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50183892) — 112pts — the "open-source alternative to X" framing for agent orchestration is new and worth watching; signals that Anthropic's managed offering is now prominent enough to clone

**Deep dives**:
- [Node2Shell evolves to HTTP2Shell, blast radius affects many HTTP libs and apps](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50219177) — read today because it expands a known attack surface to HTTP/2 and touches libraries you almost certainly depend on
- [Twelve years of distributed SQLite](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50217500) — a rare long-arc retrospective on production distributed SQLite; the lessons on consistency tradeoffs are directly applicable to anyone building local-first apps in 2026
- [How close can pure functional programming get to C?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50218195) — the benchmark methodology is unusually rigorous and the gap is smaller than most people expect; timely given the ongoing Goose language conversation on the same front page
