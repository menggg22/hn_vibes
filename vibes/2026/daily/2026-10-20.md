The builders are busy; the philosophers are busier.

## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Time-series gets its foundation model moment with Timestral, and the diffusion-at-scale story continues with Mercury 3 — the frontier is quietly expanding beyond text/image into every signal domain. Meanwhile Llmspeed staying in momentum suggests on-device ML hardware decisions are being made in real time by real buyers.
- [Timestral: Mistral's time-series foundation models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50150434) — 531pts — if Mistral can do for time-series what they did for text, every industrial sensor dataset becomes an LLM problem; worth watching what fine-tuning looks like

**agent-systems**: The agent accountability theme is the story of the day — from the Ask HN thread to Sphinx to Laminar, builders are discovering that agents produce outputs but not understanding. The RuneScape benchmark framing (MMOs as agent eval environments) is a clever research angle that sidesteps the usual "too narrow" benchmark criticism.
- [RuneScape Bench: MMOs for Agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50136126) — 138pts — MMOs have persistent state, economy, social dynamics, and partial observability; as an eval environment this is genuinely harder than most academic benchmarks

**Deep dives**:
- [If you don't run your own LLM proxy, how do you know what's going on?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50145507) — 292pts 207c — the operational security angle on LLM usage that most teams are skipping; read before your next vendor renewal
- [The obscure trust looming over Anthropic's IPO](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50151376) — 129pts 99c — the governance structure that could let Anthropic's mission override shareholder returns; unusually important corporate structure piece ahead of what will be a closely watched offering
- [Ontology-Driven Observability: Building an End-to-End Knowledge Graph at Netflix](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50153431) — 119pts — knowledge graphs as the connective tissue for observability at scale; a rare "we actually deployed this" paper from a team that would know
