## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: On-device inference is quietly becoming a product category, not just a research curiosity — Desert Ant Labs shipping local fast models and Qwen 3.8 tracking GPT prefill behavior both suggest the edge inference stack is maturing fast enough to shadow cloud APIs. The question shifting from "can it run locally?" to "how close is it to the frontier?"
- [Desert Ant Labs: local, fast models that run on device](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49624823) — 375pts — Worth examining what latency and context-length claims they're making; on-device benchmarks are still a wild west.

**agent-systems**: The "Claude, change the button" post is the day's most revealing agent story — it shows agentic e-commerce manipulation working at demo scale, and the 388-comment thread is full of people working out the trust, liability, and detection implications. Procedural/self-evolving agent graphs are also surfacing as an architectural pattern.
- [Claude, change the "Add to Cart" button to blue](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49623754) — 968pts — The real story isn't the demo; it's whether platform operators can detect or prevent this, and nobody has a good answer yet.
- [Procedural Graphs: Self-Evolving Execution Structures for LLM Agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49629868) — 40pts — Low score but architecturally interesting; self-modifying execution graphs are the next step past static DAG orchestrators.

**Deep dives**:
- [Navier-Stokes – Tristan Buckmaster](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49605915) — Read the thread before the paper; HN has pulled in people who can actually evaluate the claim, and the debate about whether the singularity is physical or a coordinate artifact is running in real time.
- [GPT-6 Astra, looped transformers, and hidden reasoning](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49627370) — The "looped transformers" angle is the genuinely novel piece here — recurrent-style depth at inference time is a meaningful architectural departure from vanilla scaling.
- [How GPT‑5.6 Sol helps run quantum computing experiments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49622561) — Concrete domain application of frontier LLMs to experimental science; the interesting question is where the model is actually adding value vs. acting as a fancy script runner.
