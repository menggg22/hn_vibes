## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: On-device inference is quietly graduating from demo to product — a 125M piano model running locally is not a research paper, it's a shipped thing you can play with. Meanwhile DiffusionGemma signals that Google is diffusing the diffusion paradigm into its own model family, which will have infrastructure consequences.

- [I trained a 125M model to autocomplete piano on-device](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49373456) — 478pts — what does the minimum viable on-device creative model actually look like in 2026? This is a concrete answer.
- [DiffusionGemma Technical Report](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49374287) — 127pts — worth reading to understand where Google's diffusion-LM research is heading and what it implies for inference infrastructure

**agent-systems**: The framing wars around AI "reasoning" are intensifying — two posts directly challenge how practitioners talk about what models are doing internally, which matters because the language shapes the architecture decisions that follow.

- [Stop Anthropomorphizing Intermediate Tokens as Reasoning/Thinking Traces](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49360140) — 189pts — if intermediate tokens aren't reasoning, what evaluation frameworks and agent designs are built on a false premise?
- [Vomit: Clean up Claude 5's token output with a separate LLM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49375996) — 169pts — using one LLM to filter another's output is already an agent pattern; the fact that someone named it "Vomit" tells you how they feel about Claude 5's verbosity

**Deep dives**:
- [Malicious Rust crate Arrayref runs a build-time payload](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49374269) — supply chain attacks in Rust are rarer and thus more alarming when they happen; build-time execution is the specific vector to understand here
- [CIA funding helped keep NeXT afloat in the 80s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49368886) — the lineage from NeXT to macOS to every Apple device is well-known; the CIA thread is not — worth reading for the institutional history
