**Top themes**: AI transparency, privacy erosion, on-device ML, open web decay, language tooling

**Vibe**: HN is simultaneously thrilled by a wave of capable local/on-device models and deeply anxious about what AI is doing to the open web, civil liberties, and the humans who once kept ethics guardrails in place at major labs.

**Standout**: [As AI eats the web, the internet's collective memory is disappearing](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49250836) — topped the energy chart with nearly 750k combined engagement units; HN is grieving something real here — 861pts 866c

## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The story today is inference everywhere — Apple Silicon getting native H3-metal and llama.cpp speedups, a 14MB agentic LLM targeting wearables, and a 2.6B model punching above its weight class. The direction is unmistakable: the frontier is moving to the edge and the margin.
- [Apple Silicon and macOS VMs: Faster LLM Inference with llama.cpp](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49259339) — 278pts — concrete benchmark numbers on a platform millions of developers already own; the VM angle is the underexplored detail worth digging into
- [H3-metal – Native MiniMax-H3 inference for Apple Silicon](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49252179) — 422pts — native Metal implementation for a new architecture; signals that the Apple Silicon ML ecosystem is maturing beyond just llama.cpp wrappers
- [LFM2.5 2.6B model competitive with 4x larger models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49173107) — 152pts — if the efficiency claims hold, this is the kind of model that makes on-device deployment a realistic default rather than a constrained fallback

**agent-systems**: The meta-story is trust and transparency — how do you know what a reasoning agent actually did, what it leaked, and whether it labeled its outputs? The "stealing reasoning traces" paper and Claude's content-marking post together frame a field that is shipping faster than its accountability layer.
- [Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257876) — 464pts — demonstrates that chain-of-thought reasoning can be reconstructed from observable API behavior; has immediate implications for any company treating CoT as a proprietary moat
- [How Claude marks AI-generated content](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49250109) — 416pts — Anthropic laying out a provenance/watermarking approach right as the "AI eating the web" story peaks; read these two together
- [WorldClaw Agentic 3D open-world generation at scale](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49265051) — 71pts — early ranking, relatively low score suggests the community hasn't fully processed it yet; worth watching if it climbs tomorrow

**Deep dives**:
- [Compression is prediction](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49263497) — foundational framing that keeps resurfacing whenever LLMs are in the discourse; read it today as context for why reasoning trace extraction works
- [What I learned by putting GitHub Copilot behind a MitM proxy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49256057) — 150pts — empirical look at what Copilot actually sends over the wire; pairs naturally with the reasoning-trace theft paper as a "what does your AI tool leak" double-bill
- [Learning more about Claude's mathematical capabilities](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49247070) — 262pts — Anthropic's own capability analysis; useful calibration data point given that Mojo 1.0 and the Go-for-AI thread are both implicitly making claims about where reasoning falls down in code generation
