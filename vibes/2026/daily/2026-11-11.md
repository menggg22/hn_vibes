## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The gap between cloud-dependent AI and locally-runnable models is narrowing fast — Apple's new local AI app, AlphaProof Code, and AMD Zen 6 all point toward inference moving to the edge. The question is whether the tooling catches up before the cloud providers lock in habits.
- [Apple has new Mac app coming to make running local AI models easy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50446520) — 100pts — Apple owning the local inference UX layer would be the most consequential on-device ML move since Core ML; worth watching what model formats they bless

**agent-systems**: Agent coordination is emerging as the real unsolved layer — Aisync, the context management Ask HN, and the "fragile codebase" piece all point to the same gap: agents work well in isolation and break down in systems. The community is building patches faster than frameworks.
- [AI usage generates a fragile codebase](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50438318) — 361pts — the most practically important piece on the front page today; documents the second-order effect of agents optimizing for local correctness over systemic coherence

**Deep dives**:
- [AlphaProof Code](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50439193) — 573pts — formal verification meets LLM code generation; worth reading to understand where AI-assisted correctness proofs are actually landing
- [Synaptics patent claims stop work on DisplayLink drivers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50443824) — 284pts — a case study in how IP law kills open hardware ecosystems; directly relevant to anyone building on USB display tech
- [An Open Field Guide to Inference Engineering](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50443714) — 68pts — low score belies practical value; a rare piece that treats inference as an engineering discipline with its own craft, not just a deployment checkbox
