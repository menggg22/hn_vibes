## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Two new model releases (Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemma-4-26B-A4B) landed within hours of each other, and the community is treating them very differently — Kimi gets excitement for capability, Gemma gets respect for efficiency. The trend toward smaller, faster inference is becoming the dominant frame even on big-model days.
- [Gemma-4-26B-A4B with 39-69% less thinking at same accuracy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50209907) — 98pts — "same accuracy, less compute" is now the headline that lands; worth reading to understand where the efficiency frontier actually sits in late 2026

**agent-systems**: The WorldTUI project (360M model pretending to be a terminal) and the OpenAI 100-companies Ask HN together tell the same story from opposite ends — agents can convincingly *simulate* autonomy at the interface layer, but nobody trusts them to run a business. The gap between demo and deployment remains the defining tension.
- [WorldTUI: I trained a 360M model to pretend to be a terminal](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50190363) — 608pts — a tiny model doing convincing terminal emulation raises uncomfortable questions about what "understanding" a system actually requires

**Deep dives**:
- [UUIDv7 doesn't have the properties you think it does](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50211746) — if you've adopted UUIDv7 for sortability and monotonicity, this is the corrective read before you build ordering logic on a shaky assumption
- [Remove pgrust: the entry is cheating (per-query kernels and result caching)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50205816) — a benchmark disqualification that reveals how far people will push query-level kernel tricks; the actual techniques described are worth stealing
- [How does Fable 5.1 hide messages in images?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50201445) — steganography in a shipping game engine in 2026 is unexpected; the implementation details are clean and the implications for IP watermarking are real
