## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The distillation-and-serve angle is heating up — multiple builders this week are attacking the cost layer rather than the capability layer, which suggests the community believes frontier quality is achievable cheaply if you're clever about knowledge transfer. On-device and SBC-class hardware appears repeatedly (CCTV on SBCs, ESP32 radar, ThinkPad-as-phone) suggesting the pendulum is swinging back toward local compute.
- [Show HN: Distill and serve small models with frontier quality for half the cost](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063454) — 13pts — the "half the cost" claim with zero comments is either very new or very niche; worth watching whether the methodology holds up to scrutiny

**agent-systems**: The agent tooling cluster today skews heavily toward *control* — multiplexers, plan annotators, guardrails — rather than new capabilities. This is a maturity signal: builders who've actually run agents in production are now solving the "how do I not lose track of what they're doing" problem.
- [Wmux – A workspace multiplexer for AI agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49056854) — 10pts — the tmux metaphor applied to agents is conceptually clean; if agent parallelism becomes standard workflow, session management becomes critical infrastructure

**Deep dives**:
