## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The on-device inference wave is hitting an inflection — three separate Show HNs today push capable models onto phones, wearables, and FPGAs, while Muse Glimmer's 30B local-agent model tops the front page. The interesting tension is whether "local" means privacy-preserving or just latency-optimized.
- [Show HN: A tiny LLM running at 21,000 tok/s on a $250 FPGA (Live Demo)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49242475) — 40pts — worth reading for the architecture choices: what had to be sacrificed to hit that token rate on commodity hardware

**agent-systems**: Sandboxing and isolation are consolidating as the critical infrastructure layer for agents — Docker Sandboxes leads the board, and Ante's offline single-binary framing suggests builders want agents that *can't* phone home even if compromised. The Muse Glimmer thread likely debates what "always-on" means for battery and privacy.
- [Docker Sandboxes – Disposable, isolated sandboxes for AI agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49239751) — 622pts — the market signal here is that agent sandboxing is no longer a nice-to-have; 347 comments suggests practitioners are stress-testing the security model

**Deep dives**:
- [Mistral Patent for "Code implemented tool calls"](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243397) — if a European open-weights lab can patent an agent tool-calling interface, every agent framework builder needs to read the claims carefully today
- [Illinois Just Passed a Law That Puts Linux on the Hook for Age Verification](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49249150) — the legal theory that an OS distribution could be liable for age-gating is genuinely novel and scary; thread likely contains the best early legal analysis
