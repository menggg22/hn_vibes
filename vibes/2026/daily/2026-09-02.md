## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The dominant story today is model efficiency at the edge — running enormous models on consumer silicon through creative memory tricks is becoming a repeatable discipline, not a one-off stunt. The "I trained a small transformer in 1.5hrs and it beats many LLMs" post crystallizes the counternarrative: careful training beats scale.
- [Show HN: Running 104GB Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on 48GB Mac with at ~12 tok/s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49524447) — 130pts — worth digging into *which* technique made the 2× RAM overhang work; that's the transferable insight
- [I trained a small transformer in 1.5hrs and it beats many LLMs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49519939) — 552pts — the benchmark claims will be contested but the training recipe is what actually matters here

**agent-systems**: MCP is solidifying as infrastructure-layer plumbing — Mcptunnels treats it as a first-class network citizen. Meanwhile the Claude Code commit-link annotation thread shows agent systems are starting to leave durable traces in developer workflows, which is a new behavioral shift worth tracking.
- [Show HN: Mcptunnels – ngrok for MCP with basic OAuth](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49527807) — 12pts — early but the "ngrok for MCP" framing signals the ecosystem is maturing past toy demos
- [I used Fable to rewrite 65kLoC of Go in Rust. It cost $400](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49526131) — 40pts — concrete cost/output data point for agentic code transformation at real scale; rare ground truth

**Deep dives**:
- [Io_uring Without Readahead](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49521623) — low-level async I/O optimization that matters directly for high-throughput inference servers; the "without readahead" angle is counterintuitive and worth understanding
- [Atlas: A World Model for Spatial Intelligence](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49525160) — world models for spatial reasoning are the quiet frontier under the LLM noise; this is worth reading before it becomes obvious
- [Path to Astra: critical capabilities and frontier safeguards](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49527595) — Google's Astra capability roadmap; read alongside the Zitron predictions thread to calibrate your own timeline confidence
