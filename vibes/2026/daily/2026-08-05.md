## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The compression frontier is moving fast — ternary weights, 4-bit quantization, and SIMD-optimized decoders are converging to make "laptop/phone-class inference" a real category rather than a demo trick. The DeepSeek V4 Flash single-GPU result and the Maple-Preview iPhone deployment suggest the community is actively stress-testing whether cloud inference is even necessary for capable models.
- [DeepSeek V4 Flash on a Single AMD MI300X](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49166386) — 362pts — worth reading for the actual throughput numbers and what they imply about AMD's competitive position against NVIDIA for inference workloads

**agent-systems**: The Warp Agent CLI and Jido Assembly both shipped this week, and the Keyv supply chain attack (named "Shai-Hulud" — someone has taste) directly targeted npm packages used in agent toolchains, making the security surface of agentic pipelines suddenly very concrete. Cloudflare also dropped both a wallet product for agents and an AI-enforced engineering standards post on the same day, which is either visionary or a lot.
- [The Warp Agent CLI](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49171766) — 92pts — curious whether this positions Warp as an agent orchestration layer or just a prettier shell; the framing will determine its ceiling

**Deep dives**:
- [When AI Benchmarks Plateau: A Systematic Study of Benchmark Saturation](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49170915) — pairs perfectly with today's "LLMs reward expertise" discourse; if benchmarks are saturating, what does progress even mean now
