*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The day's clearest infrastructure story is the distributed/idle-hardware angle — builders are treating spare Apple Silicon as a resource pool rather than waiting for cloud capacity, while on-device inference is becoming a product differentiator (not just a privacy checkbox). The Gemini 4 Argon release for robotics suggests inference is moving aggressively to edge.
- [A Dynamic and Distributed Virtual GPU Cloud Built on Idle Apple Silicon Devices](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50070593) — 216pts — worth digging into whether the coordination layer can handle heterogeneous M-chip generations; this is the hard part nobody talks about
- [Gemini 4 Argon for Robotics](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50074869) — 175pts — what latency and memory profile does on-robot inference actually require; the robotics deployment constraints are very different from cloud serving

**agent-systems**: The Anthropic source-control redesign post is the sleeper agent-systems story — it implies that long-context AI workflows are breaking the assumptions of git-based review. Simultaneously, Agent37's sandbox infrastructure launch signals the market is maturing past "can we run agents" to "can we run agents cheaply and safely."
- [Redesigning source control at Anthropic](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50073633) — 481pts — the gap between "git was designed for humans committing code" and "AI is making thousands of changes an hour" is a real infrastructure crisis waiting to happen

**Deep dives**:
- [11 year old unsoundness bugs found in Rust borrow checker](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50069499) — if Rust's core safety guarantee has had holes for 11 years the implications for "safe systems" marketing are significant; read the actual CVE thread
