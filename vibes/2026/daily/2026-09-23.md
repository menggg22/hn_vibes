## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The MiMo v2.6 story cracking 1000+ points alongside the 8GB VRAM Mini-AGI Show HN points to a consistent theme: HN is tracking the democratization frontier closely, caring as much about what runs on commodity hardware as what runs in the datacenter. The AMD random-number-generator bug story adds a cautionary note — low-level hardware assumptions are quietly biting ML workloads.

- [MiMo v2.6](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49792730) — 1091pts — worth reading for community sentiment: is excitement about a non-OpenAI/Anthropic frontier model a sign of genuine competition or hopeful projection?

**agent-systems**: Multiple fresh Show HNs shipping around agent observability, credential isolation, and parallel sandboxing in the same 24-hour window suggests a builder cohort that has moved past "build the agent" to "operate the agent safely." The MCP fork (OpenMCP) is the most structurally significant: protocol fragmentation at the tool-integration layer could have lasting ecosystem consequences.

- [Show HN: OpenMCP – An open, code-first fork of the Model Context Protocol](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49802776) — 5pts — early and low-engagement but potentially high-stakes; worth bookmarking to see if it gains traction as a standard-setting effort
