## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The inference layer is fragmenting fast — Vulkan-based runtimes, CPU-only tiny models, and open-weight decision models are all shipping simultaneously, suggesting the ecosystem is moving past "can we run it?" toward "what's the minimum viable substrate?". The hidden SDR capabilities story on ESP32 is a reminder that commodity hardware keeps surprising researchers.
- [Various Projects Find Hidden SDR Capabilities in ESP32 Microcontrollers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49922674) — 152pts — if radio capabilities are hiding in a $3 chip, what other latent compute is sitting in deployed embedded hardware waiting to be unlocked?

**agent-systems**: Agentic infrastructure is maturing into plumbing — routing, identity management, and event streams are the new primitives, moving the conversation from "can agents do tasks?" to "how do we govern, route, and audit them at scale?". Figma blocking Pi from MCP access is the first visible platform-vs-agent territorial dispute of the cycle.
- [Figma restricts MCP access to whitelisted clients, excluding Pi](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49922729) — 171pts — the first major platform gatekeeping move against an AI agent client; sets a precedent for who controls the tool-use layer

**Deep dives**:
- [Automatic Transmission – a data-privacy study of connected vehicles](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49926628) — read alongside today's border phone search story; the two together paint a picture of ambient data collection that persists regardless of what you delete
