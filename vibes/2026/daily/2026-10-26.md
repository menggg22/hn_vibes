## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Commodity pricing pressure is now visible and acute — 80% LLM price cuts in months, Cerebras deploying trillion-parameter models, and a decade-old CNN beating frontier models on a medical benchmark. The infra story is shifting from "can we run it?" to "what does it actually cost and what does it actually do?"
- [Cerebras Deploys Trillion-Parameter AI Models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50227356) — 348pts — Spark: if wafer-scale compute makes trillion-parameter inference routine, what does "frontier" even mean as a moat?

**agent-systems**: The agent layer is fracturing into trust/security concerns (AgentTrust, OpenAI Dots SSL interception question) and memory/continuity problems (MuscleMemory), suggesting the field is hitting the same walls enterprise software always hits: auditability and state management.
- [Show HN: Junior – an open-source AI junior engineer that runs on your Mac](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50233491) — 3pts — Spark: local-first agent execution reframes the privacy debate — what happens to agent adoption if the answer is simply "don't send it to the cloud"?

**Deep dives**:
- [A 10-year-old CNN outperforms frontier LLMs for skin cancer diagnosis](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50233779) — Read this alongside the Cerebras trillion-parameter story; the gap between parameter count and domain performance has never looked wider.
- [The State of Context Engineering](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50235780) — "Context engineering" is becoming the term that prompt engineering wasn't; worth understanding before it becomes interview jargon.
- [All you need is Wide Events, not "Metrics, Logs and Traces"](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50234329) — A genuine architectural argument against the observability stack orthodoxy; relevant as agent systems generate entirely new telemetry patterns.
