## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Efficiency is the story — the Energy-Based Reasoners paper claiming 385× more efficient inference is getting serious traction, and the Trainium inference engine Show HN is a sign that AWS's custom silicon ecosystem is maturing enough for open-source tooling to emerge around it. The gap between "run it in the cloud" and "run it on your hardware" is narrowing fast.
- [Energy-Based Reasoners: 385x more efficient inference](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50543989) — 300pts — 385× is an extraordinary claim; worth reading to understand whether this is architecture-level or workload-specific, and whether it trades accuracy

**agent-systems**: Two distinct signals today — enterprise validation (AI agents beating KPMG accountants in audit trials) and infrastructure maturation (managed cloud for production agents, local-first runtimes). The question is shifting from "can agents do the task?" to "how do you operate them reliably at scale?"
- [AI agents ask better questions than KPMG accountants in audit trial](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50547777) — 344pts — the specific framing of "ask better questions" rather than "give better answers" is worth unpacking; auditing is about probing, not just retrieving

**Deep dives**:
- [Quad9's withdrawal from France: A canary in the coal mine](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50538994) — read alongside the DNS piracy post (586pts) for a complete picture of how DNS infrastructure is becoming a geopolitical battleground in real time
