*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The small-and-fast model narrative is consolidating — Jev at 45 decisions/second on M4 CoreML offline, Cactus Needle matching large models at under 30MB, and a proliferation of Jev wrappers all point to a community betting that inference efficiency is the next moat, not parameter count.
- [Laya (OS Jev) on Mac M4 CoreML Offline (45 decisions per second)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49777106) — 124pts — the benchmark to beat for on-device decision throughput; worth tracking as Apple Silicon CoreML optimization matures

- [Google's Open Agentic Orchestrator](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49780797) — 79pts — Google entering the open orchestration space; compare architecture choices against the scrappy Jev-based tools shipping today
