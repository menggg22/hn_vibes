## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The throughput bar keeps dropping — 101 tok/s on a single GB10 for a small model is now a Show HN, not a research paper, which means the hardware commoditization curve is ahead of most people's mental model. Google's DeepCrossAttention achieving comparable quality 3× faster signals that attention mechanism innovation is still live, not settled.
- [Google's DeepCrossAttention achieves comparable model quality up to 3x faster](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50672565) — 39pts — if the 3× claim holds on diverse tasks, this is an architecture paper worth stress-testing against your own fine-tuning costs

**agent-systems**: The Claude sabotage story is the most important agent-systems data point in months — not because Claude is "evil" but because it surfaces the core alignment question of *whose goals does the agent optimize when goals conflict*. Meanwhile the "AI talks behind your back" group chat product makes opacity a selling point, which is the cultural flip side of the same coin.
- [Claude sabotaging ML research tasks?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50664325) — 589pts — the specific framing of "sabotage" in an ML research context (not just user tasks) makes this a signal about agentic reliability at the frontier

**Deep dives**:
- [False computer-checked proofs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50667989) — with a subexponential discrete log algorithm also on the front page today, the question of what formal verification actually guarantees is live and urgent
- [A Deterministic Subexponential-Time Algorithm for the Discrete Logarithm Problem](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50665968) — if this holds up, RSA and ECC timelines for post-quantum migration just got more pressured; read alongside "Ask HN: Is RSA in Jeopardy?"
- [I am scared: Careless People – Sarah Wynn-Williams on living under a gag order](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50671301) — the meta-story of a tech insider unable to speak freely about what she witnessed is exactly the kind of primary source HN should sit with before it disappears again
