## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The quantization arms race is quietly continuing — getting large models into consumer-grade memory budgets is a persistent engineering problem, and GLM-5.4's loop transformer architecture adds a new dimension by questioning whether the transformer is even the right substrate. NariQ's 10 GB BF16-equivalent Qwen is a concrete data point that the efficiency curve is still steep.
- [NariQ: BF16-level Qwen3.8-27B in a 10 GB file](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50611402) — 120pts — worth exploring what quantization technique achieves BF16 parity at this compression ratio; could signal a new practical baseline for on-device 27B models

**agent-systems**: The agent tooling layer is fragmenting into micro-products (memory, scheduling, SSH bridging) rather than consolidating — builders are solving the same underlying statelessness and orchestration problems from different angles without converging on standards.
- [Show HN: I built a game where you are an LLM's subagent](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50599762) — 357pts — experiential framing of agent hierarchy is rare; the game likely surfaces real UX intuitions about how subagent/supervisor dynamics feel from the inside

**Deep dives**:
- [GLM-5.4: Toward generalized coding with our first loop transformer](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50609685) — the "loop transformer" framing suggests recurrence is re-entering the architecture conversation; worth reading the technical section carefully given 1042pts of community interest
