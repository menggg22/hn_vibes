## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Three major model drops in one day (GLM-5.3, Gemini 3.7 Flash, Qwen 3.8 27B, GPT-5.6 Sol) suggests the release cadence has fully decoupled from any natural rhythm — every week is a new frontier, and HN is processing them in parallel rather than sequentially. The P2P swarm inference angle from Lumabri hints that the next frontier is escaping cloud dependency entirely.
- [Accelerating GPT-5.6 Sol Ultrafast](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49289844) — 695pts — "ultrafast" inference optimization is the new benchmark; worth reading for what latency numbers they're publishing and whether the technique generalizes

**agent-systems**: The terminal-native agent (Mole) and the URL-shareable agent (HashAgent via WebGPU) point in opposite directions — one bets on local power-user workflows, the other on zero-install shareability — and together they define the current design space tension. The Anthropic Risk report landing quietly at 51pts suggests the safety discourse is present but not dominating.
- [Show HN: Mole – Deep research agent for your terminal](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49303046) — 42pts — CLI agent that does deep research; the interesting question is how it handles source attribution vs. hallucination in a terminal context with no rich UI

**Deep dives**:
- [Going Dark, and the era of law enforcement hacking](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49304447) — GLM-5.3's "emergent cyber capabilities" headline makes this piece on law enforcement hacking essential reading today; the two stories are in direct conversation
