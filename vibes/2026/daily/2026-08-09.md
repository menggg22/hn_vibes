## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The compression and efficiency frontier is moving faster than cloud pricing can respond — running 80B models in 4GB and DeepSeek dropping another flash model suggests inference costs are becoming a solved problem at the edge, which terrifies cloud incumbents. The Postgres 300x analytics piece is adjacent: everyone is fusing ML and data pipelines closer to metal.
- [Making Postgres 300x faster for analytics: batching, operator fusion, and SIMD](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208535) — 323pts — the techniques (operator fusion, SIMD) are the same ones making LLM inference fast; read this as a general systems paper

**agent-systems**: Agents are graduating from demos to ops problems — cost management at scale, session continuity, and multi-agent communication are the new hard parts. The Claude-finds-your-phone story is charming but the "message your other Claude Code sessions" tool is the more significant signal.
- [Kitesurf: Agent-first browser that runs in V8 isolates](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208393) — 212pts — V8 isolates as agent sandboxes is an architectural bet worth watching; cheapest isolation layer that actually works

**Deep dives**:
- [Hardware backdoors in some x86 CPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49219508) — 331pts — if true at scale this rewrites supply chain threat models; read before the story gets buried by AI noise
