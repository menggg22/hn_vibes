## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The inference hardware conversation is maturing from "GPUs are scarce" to "the stack is bifurcating" — local models running at useful speeds on commodity RAM, while cloud inference is quietly regionalized and apparently fragile (see: AWS Mideast outage). On-device ML is no longer theoretical; it's the substrate for hardware projects like the bird frame.
- [The Inference Hardware Revolution of 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49713024) — 98pts — a good moment to read what the community thinks has actually changed versus what's marketing; the low comment count suggests more nodding than arguing
- [Show HN: Warp – Run DeepSeek v4.1 Flash with 5 GB of RAM at 3.77 tok/s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49714036) — 5pts — early traction but the RAM floor is the real headline; 5 GB puts capable LLM inference on most laptops sold in the last four years

**agent-systems**: The agent-tooling layer is congesting — three separate Show HNs today address the same "how do I manage agent tasks/state/output" problem, and none of them interoperate. The category is pre-standard, which means either consolidation or fragmentation is imminent.
- [Introducing System One Models and Jev](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49717558) — 635pts — the day's most-discussed AI release; "System One" framing (fast/intuitive vs. slow/deliberate) suggests a new architecture narrative is being staked out
- [Show HN: Ordewell – turn one goal into an ordered plan of coding-agent tasks](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49712276) — 48pts — planning decomposition for agents is the unsexy but necessary layer nobody has nailed yet
