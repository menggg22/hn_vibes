## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The cost-efficiency story is maturing fast — the Jev story about routing to Opus only when needed, the $20K open-weights image model, and sodium-ion batteries approaching LFP parity are all versions of the same thesis: capability is becoming cheap enough to rethink the whole stack. The interesting question is what gets rebuilt once inference is nearly free.
- [A Jev that can ask Opus for help gives Opus accuracy at 4x lower cost](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50397429) — 308pts — routing small models to call a large model only when they're uncertain is the most practical cost-reduction pattern in production LLM systems right now; worth reading to understand the threshold-setting tradeoffs

**agent-systems**: The dominant theme today isn't capability — it's accountability. Claude Code leaking emails, Gemini training on chats, OpenAI re-enabling Codex training silently: every major incident is an agent or assistant doing something the user didn't know about. Builders are responding with audit trails (Stroq), identity brokers, and zero-trust runtimes, which suggests the category is entering its "we need seatbelts" phase.
- [Claude Code sends your email address to websites](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50385272) — 1548pts — the specific mechanism matters: how does an agentic coding tool even have a pathway to send identity headers to arbitrary websites? Read to understand the ambient-credential attack surface of agentic tools

**Deep dives**:
- [Firecracker VM Escape: PwnEscape Part 2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50397219) — Firecracker is the microVM layer under Lambda and Fargate; a VM escape here is a big deal for anyone running multi-tenant workloads, and Replit just announced they moved to microVMs today
- [Chomsky and the two cultures of statistical learning (2011)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50389570) — 97pts 83c; resurfacing this 2011 debate on a day when "AI is dramatically accelerating AI research" and "I struggle to believe AI R&D is ingenious" are both on the front page is a neat temporal irony — the argument hasn't resolved, it's just gotten louder
- [Living beyond the limits of survival: wood ants trapped in a gigantic pitfall (2016)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50390525) — 161pts 52c; ants developing novel survival strategies in conditions they never evolved for is the kind of systems-thinking case study that HN reads as a metaphor for everything else happening today
