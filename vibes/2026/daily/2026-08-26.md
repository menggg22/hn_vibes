## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The Xiaomi CPU benchmark and OpenAI's Jalapeño chip story together signal a hardware arms race moving fast enough that last year's inference bottlenecks may dissolve faster than software catches up. On-device is no longer speculative — it's a Raspberry Pi in a car dashboard.
- [OpenAI Jalapeño: Better than Nvidia Blackwell](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434378) — 279pts — if the perf claims survive scrutiny, this is the first credible signal that OpenAI's vertical silicon strategy has teeth, not just marketing
- [Xiaomi: New CPU matches Apple cores single threaded, much faster multithreaded](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49420873) — 979pts — the multithreaded gap is the more interesting number: what workloads does that unlock that Apple's current lineup can't touch efficiently?
- [Cross-vendor byte-identical inference for a 72B LLM (AMD MI300X vs. Nvidia H100)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49440102) — 4pts — buried but important: reproducibility across GPU vendors is an unsolved problem that matters enormously for regulated AI deployments

**agent-systems**: Multi-agent orchestration UX is the new frontier — builders are hitting the wall of "I have five agents running, I have no idea what any of them are doing." The Show HN cluster around MulmoTerminal and audit trails signals this pain is acute right now.
- [Show HN: MulmoTerminal – Run many Claude Code sessions, see which needs you](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439218) — 4pts — attention routing is the unsolved UX problem of agentic coding; whoever gets this right ships the "tmux for agents" that everyone will use
- [Behaviorally fingerprinting Ox Alpha's provenance](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49435641) — 27pts — probing an AI agent's training lineage through behavior is a nascent but important security/attribution technique worth following

**Deep dives**:
