**Top themes**: AI fatigue & fragmentation, systems reliability, open-source tooling, security, writing/craft

**Vibe**: HN is in a deeply ambivalent mood about AI — celebrating what cheaper/local models can do while openly questioning whether the big expensive ones are worth it; simultaneously, the crowd is retreating to timeless craft (writing, complex systems, harnesses) as a counterweight to the noise.

## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Local and open models are visibly eating into the commercial API narrative — not just in benchmarks but in real shipped work. The "why your local LLM feels dumber than it is" piece (472pts) suggests the bottleneck has shifted from capability to configuration and context management.
- [Why your local LLM feels dumber than it is](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49402232) — 472pts — the framing implies most local model underperformance is an inference/prompting artifact, not a weights problem; worth reading for the specific failure modes named

**agent-systems**: The ecosystem is fragmenting into micro-tools — memory engines, model routers, harness layers — rather than converging on a platform. GLM-5.3 finishing a real reverse-engineering job in 30 minutes (309pts) is the kind of benchmark-in-the-wild that moves people's priors faster than any paper.
- [I gave Qwen 3.8 27B a reverse-engineering job and it finished in 30 minutes](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49407507) — 309pts — concrete task completion story with a named open model; the specificity (27B, 30min) makes it credible and reproducible

**Deep dives**:
- [How Complex Systems Fail (1998)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49409473) — 222pts — resurfaces today almost certainly as subtext for Wi-Fi 8 and the Android firmware malware story; 28-year-old paper that reads like it was written about 2026 infra
- [JIT Compiling Code in 5μs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49406387) — 154pts — rare low-level performance piece with a concrete, verifiable claim; the 5μs headline is either impressive or suspicious depending on what's being compiled — read to find out
