**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The H100 price arbitrage story and TPU 8tx/8i Max launch on the same day illustrates a maturing market where compute is simultaneously commoditizing (5x price spread on identical H100s) and differentiating at the high end. On-device ML is quietly advancing — someone ran 120B MoE under 16GB on M1 Max, which a year ago would have been a headline.

- [ChatGPT Desktop forwards your SSH agent to remote machines](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422628) — 205pts — If accurate, this is a significant ambient privilege escalation; the Ask HN framing suggests it's not widely known yet
