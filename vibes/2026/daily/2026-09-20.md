## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Tiny models matching giants is the week's recurring theme — Cactus Needle 3 claiming 8-29MB models match DeepSeek V4 Flash is either the beginning of a real on-device shift or benchmark-gaming, and HN is stress-testing which. Memory efficiency is the other axis, with RAM-saving infrastructure work getting serious upvotes.
- [Show HN: Cactus Needle 3: 8-29MB automation models can match DeepSeek V4 Flash](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49748553) — 225pts — if the benchmark holds under scrutiny, this is the on-device inflection point people have been waiting for
- [Saving another 100TB of RAM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49758580) — 461pts — unglamorous infrastructure work that directly enables cheaper model serving at scale
- [Cache-to-Cache: Direct Semantic Communication Between LLMs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49758615) — 102pts — early-stage but the idea of LLMs communicating via KV-cache rather than token strings could meaningfully change multi-agent latency

**agent-systems**: Computer-use agents are getting their own model class now — CUA-S1's "System One" framing suggests the field is maturing past "use GPT-4 with a screenshot" toward purpose-built fast-inference architectures. Non-autoregressive approaches are also surfacing as a serious alternative path.
- [Show HN: CUA-S1 – A System One Model for Computer Use](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49767564) — 56pts — purpose-built computer-use inference model, not a prompted general model; signals architectural specialization is arriving
- [I built non-autoregressive decision models with RL a year ago](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49765348) — 1053pts — timing vindication post from someone who was early; the non-autoregressive approach for decision/action tasks is worth understanding before it's mainstream
- [How OpenAI Used Its Own LLMs to Design Its Jalapeño Chip](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49761432) — 196pts — AI-designed silicon is no longer theoretical; the recursive loop of AI improving its own hardware is accelerating

**Deep dives**:
- [Inside ZCode: Silently uploading your Git history to the cloud](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49750694) — 327pts — if your dev toolchain is quietly exfiltrating your entire commit history, this is the security story of the week and worth reading carefully
- [Android 17 is the first since 3.x to add new APIs without releasing to the AOSP](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49758736) — 1110pts — the technical and legal implications of Google quietly closing Android's open-source surface are underreported; this thread is the best current analysis
