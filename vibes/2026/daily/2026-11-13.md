## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The on-device push is fragmenting into two camps — "make existing models smaller" and "build entirely new runtimes." Both Kiln (WebGPU) and the LLM-on-old-devices post reflect the same underlying frustration: cloud inference is too expensive and too fragile, and builders want local fallbacks that actually work.
- [I'm making LLMs work on small and old devices](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50468888) — 275pts — the specific constraint of *old* hardware (not just small) is underexplored; what's the oldest viable target today?

**agent-systems**: Self-improvement and managed orchestration are the two poles of agent architecture right now — AgentRun bets on self-rewriting logic while Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Orchestrator bets on Google owning the orchestration layer. The tension between open and proprietary agent runtimes is sharpening.
- [Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Orchestrator](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472260) — 286pts — Google shipping an *Orchestrator* product alongside the model is the strategically interesting part; it's a claim on the agent-middleware market

**Deep dives**:
- [22-Year-Old PostgreSQL Bug Turned into Remote Code Execution](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50471761) — 138pts — a two-decade-old Postgres bug achieving RCE is a must-read for anyone running self-hosted Postgres in 2026; patch status unclear from headline alone
- [Why writing correct software is hard and why math (alone) won't help us (2016)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50465352) — 354pts 166c — resurfaced today and earning comments a decade later; read alongside the Postgres RCE for a full picture of why correctness remains unsolved
- [Chinese frontier AI models carry the fingerprint of export-controlled GPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50474883) — 139pts — the forensic methodology here (fingerprinting training hardware from model outputs) is new and has obvious geopolitical implications beyond China
