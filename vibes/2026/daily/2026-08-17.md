## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The week's defining tension is between open-weight local models getting dramatically smaller (DeepSeek shrunk to 57GB, Qwen 3.8 27B runaway hit) and the commercial layer on top becoming more extractive and opaque. On-device is winning mindshare even as cloud providers lock in.
- [Qwen 3.8 27B](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299605) — 1407pts — worth reading the thread for the benchmark skepticism and the genuine user reports running it locally; the gap between frontier and local is collapsing faster than expected

**agent-systems**: Multi-agent complexity is arriving before the tooling to manage it — patterns, anti-patterns, and safety guards are all shipping simultaneously, suggesting the field is in a messy adolescence rather than a mature engineering discipline.
- [Patterns and problems in emerging multi-agent systems](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49316271) — 179pts — practical taxonomy of what's actually going wrong in production multi-agent deployments; more grounded than most agent discourse

**Deep dives**:
- [AI isn't outthinking mathematicians, it's out-remembering them](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49312845) — the memorization-vs-reasoning distinction applied to math benchmarks is the most important methodological critique of AI capability claims right now; read before citing any math benchmark
- [The AI Credit Resale Economy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49320611) — with Stripe buying OpenRouter for $7B, understanding the gray-market API credit economy explains the actual financial plumbing being acquired
