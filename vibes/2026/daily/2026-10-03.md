## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Local inference is becoming a commodity hardware question rather than a software question — Janus runs GGUF over Vulkan on any GPU, and the Redis creator's ds4 tool brings LLMs to local environments with minimal ceremony. The interesting pressure is whether on-device quality closes the gap fast enough to matter for agents.

- [From the creator of Redis; run LLM locally with ds4](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49936575) — 125pts — provenance matters here: Redis-level pragmatism applied to local LLM deployment is worth watching for API design choices

**agent-systems**: The harness layer is fragmenting into micro-opinions — human-in-the-loop vs. fully autonomous, GUI vs. CLI, quota-sharing vs. self-hosted. Every Show HN in this space is betting on a different coordination philosophy, and none has escaped velocity yet.

- [Every SaaS business will become a harness around a model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49938616) — 77pts — the thesis getting tested in real time by every other item in today's Builder Pulse; worth reading the 55 comments for the pushback

**Deep dives**:
- [Greg Kroah-Hartman – Security in the LLM Age [video]](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49929391) — the Linux kernel maintainer's security perspective on LLMs is a rare non-hype signal from someone who ships production infrastructure at scale
- [Loss of cell identity drives human aging: Two new papers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49926411) — two independent papers converging on the same mechanism makes this more than a single-study curiosity; the biological implication is significant
- [The Legend of von Neumann (1973)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49933235) — a 53-year-old piece about genius mythology is suddenly relevant when the industry is again debating what individual intelligence means vs. collective AI systems
