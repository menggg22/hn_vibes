## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The throughput race is becoming a commodity story — 607 tok/s across two DGX stations is impressive but the more interesting signal is the counter-trend: builders compressing frontier models into 200 lines of Jax or running them on old phones, suggesting the community is bifurcating between "how fast can the cluster go" and "how small can I make this."
- [Show HN: DeepSeek V3 in ~200 lines of pure Jax](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50497589) — 163pts — worth reading as a minimal reference implementation; if a model can be expressed this cleanly, what does that say about framework bloat elsewhere?

**agent-systems**: The agent builder space is in a Cambrian explosion phase with no clear winner — governance, budget-capping, browser automation, incident response, and coordination dashboards all shipped within the same 12-hour window, suggesting builders are pattern-matching from last year's LLM-wrapper wave but targeting the orchestration layer instead.
- [Show HN: Agweb – a browser for agents that stays out of your way](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50497147) — 86pts — the "browser as agent primitive" framing is the most architecturally interesting of the batch; worth watching whether this becomes infrastructure or a feature someone else absorbs

**Deep dives**:
- [Chinese frontier AI models carry the fingerprint of export-controlled GPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50474883) — 373pts/161c — connects the Nvidia scarcity/smuggling stories to a geopolitical hardware forensics angle; the idea that GPUs leave detectable fingerprints in model weights is technically fascinating and politically loaded
