## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The undocumented Mac family classifier story is the clearest signal yet that Apple is running non-trivial on-device ML models silently, without documentation or user consent — which raises both privacy and reproducibility questions. Meanwhile builders are pushing hard on compression (31B→14B diffusion) to make large models locally viable.
- [Every Mac comes with an (undocumented) family member classifier](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50403097) — 587pts — what else is Apple's Neural Engine classifying that nobody has found yet?

**agent-systems**: The "agentic setup" Ask HN thread is a live archaeology dig — 682 comments of practitioners comparing notes with no consensus tooling, suggesting the space is still pre-paradigm. The Components of Agent Memory article provides one of the cleaner conceptual frameworks available right now.
- [The Components of Agent Memory](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50402212) — 151pts — a rare structured breakdown of memory architecture in agents, useful as a reference when the Ask HN thread gets chaotic

**Deep dives**:
- [Firecracker VM Escape: PwnEscape Part 2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50397219) — 281pts — Firecracker is the foundation of Lambda and Fly.io; a VM escape here matters for anyone running multi-tenant workloads and deserves careful reading
