## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: On-device and low-latency inference is the consistent builder north star this week — sub-50ms TTS, 125M piano models, and one-command quantization tools all point to a community that has accepted cloud AI costs and latency as a problem worth engineering around rather than accepting. The Shoehorn quantization tool's continued momentum confirms that "run it on your machine" is becoming a product category.
- [How we made a text-to-speech model respond in sub-50 ms](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49389952) — 95pts — the architectural choices to hit wall-clock latency under 50ms are worth dissecting; latency is the new accuracy battle

**agent-systems**: The agent space is bifurcating between people building pipelines and people building the glue — memory, observability, and human-in-the-loop control — suggesting the pipeline layer is commoditizing fast while the reliability/trust layer is wide open. The "Codex vs Claude" comparison thread getting 80 comment engagement on a fairly low score signals strong practitioner opinion forming around which agents actually survive contact with real codebases.
- [Quick impressions: A week of using Codex more than Claude](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id:49393051) — 70pts, 80c — practitioner comparison with enough specificity to be useful; the comment-to-score ratio means people are sharing their own divergent experiences

**Deep dives**:
- [What happens when a GPU reads memory](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id:49390308) — 93pts — foundational mental model for anyone doing inference optimization; coalescing and cache behavior at this level determines real-world throughput
