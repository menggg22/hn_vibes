**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The inference optimization conversation is maturing from "can we run it locally" to "can we run it fast and correctly locally" — vLLM tool-call accuracy and NVFP4 throughput benchmarks both appeared today, suggesting practitioners are now stress-testing production edge cases rather than just demo scenarios.
- [Fixing Tool-Call Accuracy in vLLM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50242870) — 144pts — tool-call reliability is the silent blocker for real agentic deployments; worth reading to understand what "production-ready inference" actually means in 2026

The safety-capability frontier is moving fast enough that the bugs are now architectural, not just prompt-level.
- [Cross-request KV cache exposure in the GLM-5.3 Workers AI model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50252159) — 35pts — a concrete, reproducible infrastructure failure in a hosted model that leaked other users' context; the kind of bug that will define compliance conversations for the next year

**Deep dives**:
