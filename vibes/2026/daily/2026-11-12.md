## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The Apple local-AI app and Huawei's MACaQ quantizer arriving the same day suggests the "local inference as a commodity" era is genuinely here — both a tier-1 consumer platform and a sanctioned hardware adversary are shipping first-party quantization tooling simultaneously. The interesting question is whether Apple's ease-of-use push accelerates or undercuts the open-source layer.
- [Apple has new Mac app coming to make running local AI models easy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50446520) — 515pts — worth reading comments for the debate over whether Apple will sandbox model weights the way it sandboxes apps
- [Huawei releases MACaQ, its internal quantizer for local inference](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50458166) — 55pts — Huawei publishing internal tooling is rare; worth benchmarking against llama.cpp's quantization quality
- [Faster Retrieval for Better, More Efficient RAG](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50455633) — 146pts — retrieval quality is quietly the ceiling for most production RAG deployments; this deserves engineering attention
- [Attention to Mamba: A Recipe for Cross-Architecture Distillation](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50453171) — 65pts — distilling transformer knowledge into Mamba-class architectures is a live research question with real on-device implications

**agent-systems**: United Airlines banning AI agents from booking, Atuin threading agent sessions, and the Maya site discovery all landed today — the community is processing what agents actually *do* in the wild versus what they're designed to do, and institutional pushback is officially starting. The gap between agent capability and agent accountability is the story.
- [United Airlines bans AI agents from booking flights on behalf of travelers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50458900) — 325pts — read the comments for the legal liability thread; this is likely the first of many "terms of service vs. agent autonomy" battles
- [Gemini Decisions API](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50456791) — 245pts — Google shipping a dedicated "decisions" surface is an architectural signal worth unpacking
- [Atuin Threads: All your agent sessions on every machine](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50453637) — 189pts — session continuity across machines is the unsexy plumbing that multi-agent workflows actually need
- [Launch HN: Pomerado (YC F26) – Turn any website into an MCP for agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50455811) — 47pts — MCP adapter layer for arbitrary websites; if this works reliably it removes the scraping-vs-API tension

**Deep dives**:
- [My AI agents found an unmapped Maya site?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50452455) — the methodology thread will tell you more about what satellite+LiDAR+agent pipelines can actually do today than any benchmark
