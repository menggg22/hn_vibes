## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The story today is radical compression — running 70B models on a single 4GB GPU and deploying frontier-class video/audio models via open weights in ComfyUI signals that the infrastructure gap between cloud and local is closing faster than most expected. Chinese labs (Qwen, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax) are now the ones setting the pace on open-weight releases.
- [AirLLM 70B inference with single 4GB GPU](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49154228) — 182pts — worth understanding the layer-by-layer streaming technique; this is the kind of trick that becomes standard practice in 12 months
- [Smaller, faster, safer: running Kimi and GLM at scale](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49158581) — 127pts — production-scale deployment notes from teams running these models; rare operational detail
- [MiniMax H3 Day-0 Support in ComfyUI: Open Weights, Native Audio, and 2K Video](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49155629) — 242pts — the ComfyUI ecosystem as a deployment platform for open multimodal models is maturing rapidly

**agent-systems**: The community is actively probing the ceiling of what agents can autonomously complete, with skepticism rising alongside capability claims. The "meat proxy" framing is the day's dominant mental model — the risk isn't that agents fail, it's that humans approve failures without noticing.
- [What's the largest software project AI can complete on its own?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49157786) — 62pts — an empirical question the community is genuinely trying to answer; the comment thread will be more useful than the article
- [Show HN: Nightcrawler – A local AI pentesting agent running on a smartphone](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49154127) — 102pts — on-device agents for adversarial tasks is a new frontier with serious dual-use implications worth tracking
- [Launch HN: Hoplite (YC S26) – Effortlessly deploy cloud coding agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49157997) — 49pts — YC backing a coding agent deployment layer suggests the infra-for-agents market is now considered fundable at scale

**Deep dives**:
- [Ten advances in mathematics and theoretical computer science](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49157930) — 389pts, 673c — rare HN moment where a math survey generates more comments than most AI stories; read this for signal on what the CS-adjacent community actually finds intellectually alive right now
