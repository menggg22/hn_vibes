## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The RAM and compute efficiency story is maturing fast — Rust Glancer at 100× less RAM and Shoehorn's quantization-for-consumer-hardware both signal that "make it fit on my machine" is now a primary engineering goal, not an afterthought. On-device piano autocomplete at 125M params is the most vivid proof that small models with tight scope outperform bloated generalists for specific tasks.
- [Rust Glancer: Rust LSP using 100x less RAM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49393052) — 392pts — worth exploring as a template: aggressive scope reduction + one language = dramatic resource wins; asks whether other LSPs are just lazy

**agent-systems**: The agent layer is getting satirized (Munder Difflin — "office of your clones") at the same moment real harnesses like Autolith and OzBrain are shipping, which is a reliable sign the space is crossing from hype into messy productization. The MCP roadmap thread is the coordination point everyone is quietly watching.
- [Munder Difflin – Agent harness to run an office of your clones](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398152) — 242pts — the joke framing lowers defenses; the actual architecture question underneath (how do you coordinate N instances of yourself?) is genuinely hard and underexplored

**Deep dives**:
- [Anthropic appears to be A/B testing reduced effort levels in Claude Code](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49401549) — if confirmed, this is the first documented case of a major AI vendor silently degrading product quality for cost reasons; read the comment thread for receipts

- [Everyone says assembly is untyped—everyone is wrong](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49376769) — 144pts with a provocative thesis about implicit typing in register conventions; useful mental model for anyone thinking about compiler backends or verification
