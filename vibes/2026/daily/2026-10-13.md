# HN Vibes — 2026-10-13

**Top themes**: privacy/fingerprinting, AI skepticism, retro-computing nostalgia, open-source tools, passkey backlash

**Vibe**: HN is in a deeply conflicted mood about AI — simultaneously excited by what it can do and furious at how it's being shoved into everything; the fingerprinting story and passkey story are driving genuine anger about the erosion of user autonomy, and Terence Tao's Math 2.0 PDF is quietly commanding the room.

**Standout**: [Passkeys are a trap](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50048312) — 1014 points and 535 comments of sustained, passionate debate about whether the industry just swapped one lock-in for another; 1014pts 535c

## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The WiFi-7-router-as-SDR story and the Intel D270 GPU accelerator both point to the same quiet trend: capable compute is migrating to the edge in weird, non-obvious form factors. Meanwhile Janas (streaming LLM experts from SSD) represents a scrappy answer to the RAM wall that most users hit before they can run anything interesting locally.
- [Turn a WiFi 7 Router into a 320Msps Software Defined Radio Receiver](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50055593) — 242pts — What else in your home network stack is secretly a capable compute/RF platform waiting to be unlocked?

**agent-systems**: Two forces in tension today — the QGIS moratorium on AI contributions signals that open-source communities are developing immune responses to agent-generated code, while the Codex CLI daemon story shows commercial tooling quietly escalating its footprint without consent. The "agents generate more code, not more software" piece gives the theoretical frame for both.
- [AI coding agents generate more code, but not more software](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50059523) — 297pts — The distinction between code volume and software value is the most useful frame for evaluating any agent coding tool right now
- [Launch HN: Agent37 (YC F26) – Low-cost agent sandboxes for running agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50059096) — 82pts — Sandboxing is becoming the critical infrastructure layer for the agent era; worth watching what pricing model emerges

**Deep dives**:
- [Terence Tao: Math 2.0 [pdf]](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50043026) — Read this today because a serious mathematician making a first-principles argument for how AI changes mathematical practice is a different class of document than the usual AI-changes-everything takes
- [Fingerprints are the new cookies](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50048642) — 701pts — With 340 comments and still climbing, this is the privacy story of the month; the technical specifics of what browser fingerprinting can do in 2026 have changed dramatically
- [QGIS enacts moratorium on AI contributions](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50054751) — 362pts 395c — A mature, well-governed open-source project drawing a hard line is a case study in how communities will have to actively defend their epistemics against agent-generated noise
