## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The price compression story is dominating — GPT 6.1 Sol at one-fifth the cost of Astra-class models is the kind of step that changes what's viable to run in production, and TurboGPT's 13-second micro-training demo hints that even fine-tuning is moving to the edge. Jevstiller's disagreement-bound framing is interesting because it treats local distillation not as a replacement but as a confidence-calibrated approximation.
- [Show HN: TurboGPT: train 22KiB transformer in 13s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49898931) — 41pts — worth examining what they stripped out to hit 22KiB without losing useful expressivity; the constraint is the insight

**agent-systems**: Always-on agents are becoming a product category in earnest today — Dots ships persistent agents, Nvidia pushes hardware oversight, and Corral + Ctxfw ship containment primitives the same day. The infrastructure layer is bifurcating into "make agents do more" and "make agents safe to leave running," and both are shipping simultaneously.
- [Dots: Always-on agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49896604) — 444pts — the specific design choices around persistence, context window management across sessions, and failure recovery are the things to scrutinize here
- [Nvidia wants to put a watchdog chip next to every AI agent](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49879883) — 223pts — hardware-level attestation for agent actions raises the question of who controls the watchdog's policy and whether this is safety or a new platform lock-in vector

**Deep dives**:
- [A Privacy Analysis of Web and Mobile Conversational AI Agents [pdf]](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49890226) — 406pts — with DraftKings and dynamic pricing both trending today, this PDF is the empirical backbone for understanding what data conversational agents are actually exfiltrating; read together they form a complete picture
- [GLM-5.3 and the spread of advanced cyber capabilities](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49897075) — 182pts 179c — a capable open-weight model being analyzed specifically for its offensive cyber utility is the most important underreported story on today's front page; the comment-to-score ratio suggests experts are in there disagreeing
