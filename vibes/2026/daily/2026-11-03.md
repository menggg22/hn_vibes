## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The big story is weight I/O — Astra reading and writing weights to improve Qwen is the headline, but the quiet debunker ("LLMs are not superhuman at reading weights") sitting at 20pts suggests the community is not uniformly credulous. Meanwhile Claude Opus 5.5 drawing permit-level architectural drawings and an AI agent performing brain surgery on a mouse signal that inference is being pushed into extremely high-stakes physical domains.
- [LLMs are not superhuman at reading and writing neural network weights](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50344861) — 20pts — worth reading alongside the Astra story; if true, the weight-editing narrative needs significant qualification

**agent-systems**: Agentic backdoors via pretraining poisoning is the sleeper story — it's technically sober and addresses a supply-chain threat to agent deployment that most teams aren't thinking about yet. The Deedglass Show HN (agent claimed 1 row, Postgres changed 301) and AgentTrafficCop both show builders encountering agentic failure modes in production and scrambling to instrument them.
- [Agentic backdoors via pretraining poisoning](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341334) — 87pts — pretraining-time backdoors that activate only in agentic contexts are a qualitatively different threat model than prompt injection; read before deploying autonomous agents

**Deep dives**:
- [Postgres 19 is late because so many more bugs are being found now](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50312408) — 805pts 479c — the most interesting meta-story in software quality: AI-assisted fuzzing and formal methods are surfacing bugs faster than humans can fix them, stressing release cadences
- [Moving our warehouse off DuckDB](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50338747) — 158pts 107c — a concrete war story about DuckDB's limits at scale; useful counterweight to the DuckDB hype cycle
- [Egoless Mathematics in the AI Era](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341736) — 104pts 79c — philosophical piece on how AI changes attribution and credit in mathematical discovery; worth reading for anyone thinking about knowledge work identity
