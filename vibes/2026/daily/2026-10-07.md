## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Open-weight models are compressing the frontier fast — Mistral Large 4 and Beam's 501B open-weight release in the same week signal that the "only labs can run frontier" assumption is crumbling, and the hardware layer is following with OpenTPU. On-device inference is becoming a serious engineering discipline rather than a demo curiosity.
- [Beam: Reflection's 501B open-weight model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49969183) — 540pts — what does 501B open-weight mean for inference cost curves and who actually has the hardware to run it locally vs. needing cloud anyway?

**agent-systems**: Agents are moving from capability demos to production artifacts with real consequences — materials science candidates, diary entries as evidence, circuit breakers as reliability primitives. The engineering question is shifting from "can agents do X" to "how do we contain, audit, and trust them."
- [Opus 5.5 agents discover two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970667) — 482pts — the verification pipeline for AI-proposed scientific results is the genuinely hard unsolved problem here; how does peer review adapt?

**Deep dives**:
- [Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970871) — 272pts — if the training paradigm itself is changing, every assumption about compute cost and hardware design needs revisiting; read before it's cited everywhere
- [Subquadratic 3SUM and Subcubic APSP](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49977437) — 92pts — complexity theory breakthrough week alongside the AI math proofs; the juxtaposition of human and AI mathematical progress is unusually visible right now
- [Direct retinal projection display for smart glasses using a meta-optic mirror](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49944369) — 124pts — the hardware path for ambient AI interfaces depends entirely on display breakthroughs like this; worth understanding the physics before the product announcements
