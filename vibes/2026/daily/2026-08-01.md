**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The efficiency race is the dominant story — DeepSeek V4 Flash and the Gemma-in-2GB Show HN both push the same thesis that inference cost curves are collapsing faster than most roadmaps assumed. Kimi K3 on 29GB of consumer RAM adds a third data point, making this feel like a coordinated (if coincidental) pressure campaign on cloud inference pricing.
- [Run Kimi K3 using 29 GB of RAM at 0.50 tok/s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49123386) — 132pts — 0.50 tok/s is genuinely slow but the RAM ceiling keeps dropping; worth tracking the trajectory

- [Everyone is building LLM routers, we deprecated ours](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49126630) — 82pts — post-hype operator perspective; the deprecation story is rarer and more valuable than the launch story
