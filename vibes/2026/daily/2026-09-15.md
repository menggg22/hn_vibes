## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: The on-device and self-hosted ML stack is maturing visibly — Qwen3-based TTS/ASR, Ollama migration notes, and Rust-based DSP tools all appeared today, suggesting practitioners are moving past curiosity into production pain. The real signal is that preprompt migration from Opus to Ollama is generating 35kb of notes worth sharing.
- [Notes on gotchas while migrating 35kb preprompts from Opus to self-hosted Ollama](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49697014) — 108pts — real operational knowledge; the delta between cloud and local model behavior is apparently large enough to fill a small book

**agent-systems**: The agent layer is fragmenting into a thousand competing primitives — runtimes, registries, credential brokers, storage layers — without any clear winner, which explains why so many Show HNs today are solving identical sub-problems. Pion's claim to run entire companies autonomously (262pts) lands in this context as either the destination or the hype ceiling.
- [Pion, an agent designed to run any company autonomously](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49700477) — 262pts — the "full company automation" claim will draw scrutiny; interesting to watch what HN actually believes vs. upvotes

**Deep dives**:
