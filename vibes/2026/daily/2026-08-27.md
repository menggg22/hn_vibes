## 🔭 Depth — ml-infra / on-device ML / agent-systems

*What's the trend and what sparks curiosity.*

**ml-infra & on-device ML**: Two Flash-tier models (GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.8) dominating the front page on the same day signals that "small-but-capable" is now the competitive frontier — the race isn't to the largest model anymore, it's to the best quality-per-token on constrained hardware. The Hugging Face incident thread adds urgency: centralized model hosting is fragile, and the community is starting to feel it.
- [The Hugging Face incident and the road ahead](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49454314) — 141pts — what actually failed and whether it changes how teams should think about model dependency in production

**agent-systems**: The VM containment debate is the most technically substantive agent-systems story of the day, and it's skeptical rather than promotional — a useful corrective to the default assumption that sandboxing solves agent safety. Meanwhile RAG getting a "simpler than you think" treatment suggests the hype-to-understanding gap is finally closing.
- [VMs won't contain cyber-capable agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49450188) — 137pts — concrete argument that VM escapes are within reach of sufficiently capable agents, which reframes the entire "just sandbox it" school of agent deployment thinking

**Deep dives**:
- [RAG Is Simpler Than You Think](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49445727) — 423pts — high score suggests this is filling a real gap between "RAG hype" and "RAG reality"; worth reading to calibrate against your current implementation
- [Queryable Executables](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49442589) — 307pts — embedding query interfaces directly into binaries is a genuinely novel systems idea that intersects with agent tool-use and observability
- [Stalking the Wily Hacker: 40 years later – Cliff Stoll](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49395802) — 285pts — the 40-year retrospective framing makes this more than nostalgia; Stoll's original threat model looks remarkably prescient when read against today's agent-systems security debates
